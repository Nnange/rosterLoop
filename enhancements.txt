Core Features & Enhancements
    1. User Authentication & Authorization
        Allow users to create accounts and log in
        Share household rosters with other members via invite codes
        Track who created/manages the household
        Role-based permissions (admin can modify schedule, members can only view)
    2. Enhanced Schedule Management
        Manual Schedule Adjustments: Allow swaps between flatmates ("Can I swap my week with yours?")
        Skip/Excuse System: Let someone skip a week due to travel/illness with administrator approval
        Custom Rotation Rules:
        Exclude certain people from specific weeks
        Weight assignments based on frequency (some people clean more/less)
        Support bi-weekly or monthly rotations instead of just weekly
        Multi-day Assignments: Assign different rooms/areas to different people for the same weekend
    3. Notifications & Reminders
        Email/SMS reminders 48 hours before cleaning assignment
        In-app notifications dashboard
        Slack/Discord integration for notifications
        Reminder frequency customization (daily, day-before, etc.)
    4. Task Management & Checklists
        Define cleaning tasks for each rotation (bathroom, kitchen, living room, etc.)
        Checklist system to track completion
        Photo verification of completed tasks
        Task difficulty levels affecting point systems
    5. Performance & Accountability Tracking
        Completion Tracking: Mark when tasks are complete
        Ratings System: Rate flatmates' cleaning quality (1-5 stars)
        Leaderboard: Show who completes tasks on time most consistently
        Penalty System: Track missed or incomplete cleanings
        Reports: Generate monthly/quarterly cleaning performance reports
    6. Social & Gamification Features
        Points/Achievement System: Earn badges for consecutive on-time cleanings
        Streaks: Track consecutive weeks of completed tasks
        Rewards: Suggest rewards for high performers (movie night, dinner, etc.)
        Team Chat: Built-in messaging for household members
    7. Financial Integration
        Track who owes money for cleaning supplies purchased communally
        Split bills for shared household expenses
        Fine system for missed cleanings (optional)
        Expense sharing calculator
    8. Mobile App
        Native mobile app (React Native/Flutter) for quick access on-the-go
        Offline capability to check who's cleaning this week
        Push notifications directly to phone
    9. Analytics & Insights
        Dashboard showing cleaning trends
        Heatmaps of busy seasons
        Per-person statistics (avg. completion time, missed cleanings, etc.)
        Export reports as PDF/CSV
    10. Advanced Customization
        Theme/Branding: Allow households to customize colors, logo, household name
        Custom Holidays: Exclude specific dates (holidays, everyone traveling)
        Seasonal Adjustments: Different schedules for different seasons
        Import/Export: Backup and restore household data
    11. Administrative Tools
        Bulk import flatmates from CSV
        Schedule templates for common household sizes
        Audit logs of all changes made to the schedule
        Data retention and cleanup policies
    12. Integration & APIs
        Google Calendar Integration: Sync schedule with personal calendars
        Third-party Calendars: iCal export for Apple Calendar, Outlook, etc.
        Webhook Support: Trigger external actions when assignments change
        Public API: Allow other apps to read household schedules
    13. Quality of Life Features
        Dark Mode: Reduce eye strain for late-night browsing
        Multi-language Support: Translations for international users
        Accessibility: WCAG compliance, screen reader support
        Mobile Responsive: Already partially done, but full mobile optimization
    14. Conflict Resolution
        Dispute Resolution System: When someone challenges an assignment
        Voting System: Vote on schedule changes
        Comments/Notes: Add notes to specific weeks
        History Tracking: See previous schedules and changes
    15. Advanced Scheduling Algorithms
        ML-based Optimization: Learn preferences and optimize accordingly
        Fair Balancing: Ensure no one gets weekend clusters (multiple consecutive weeks)
        Preference Engine: Allow people to mark preferred/disliked dates
        Capacity Planning: Consider who's available/unavailable
